# Language tier: T1 — Clean Constituent

Write a complete, grammatically correct question in plain American English.

Requirements:
- Full sentences with proper capitalization and punctuation
- Ends with a question mark
- No abbreviations, SMS shorthand, or jargon
- Neutral, polite tone — sounds like a literate adult writing a formal inquiry or email
- One to two sentences maximum
- Does not repeat the official FAQ phrasing verbatim — rephrase naturally

Style tags to use: ["T1", "complete_sentence", "standard_grammar", "polite_register"]
