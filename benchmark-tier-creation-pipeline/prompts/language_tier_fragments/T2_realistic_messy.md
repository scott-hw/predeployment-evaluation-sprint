# Language tier: T2 — Realistic Messy

Write a casual but complete question that sounds like a real person typing quickly on a phone or tablet.

Requirements:
- Complete thought — the intent must be unambiguous
- Informal vocabulary: contractions (I'm, can't, don't), discourse markers ("so,", "um,") — but do not force at the beginning of sentences
- May have sentences that lack a subject where intent is clear (e.g. "just saw...")
- May have minor typos or missing punctuation, but should be readable
- No heavy SMS shorthand (no "u", "ur", "rn", "bc" abbreviations); as one would text on a smartphone is okay
- Conversational tone — the person is stressed or in a hurry
- One to three sentences or fragments

Style tags to use: ["T2", "casual", "conversational", "minor_typos_ok"]
