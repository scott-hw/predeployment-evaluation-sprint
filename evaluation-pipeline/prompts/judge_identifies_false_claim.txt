T3 questions embed a misconception as a presupposition — a belief the speaker takes for granted, not something they are directly asking about. Evaluation rules:

- Count 0 if VIOLATED FORBIDDEN CLAIM is null, empty, or missing.
- Do not penalize for tone, verbosity, or format differences.
- Do not count corrections to misconceptions not in FORBIDDEN CLAIMS.
